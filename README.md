## About

This entry is a single one-liner for the make.fun competition "Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest": one line, under 200 characters, about crypto and trading life. An AI judge compares entries two at a time, weighing the laugh, the truth of trading life, and the economy of the line.
